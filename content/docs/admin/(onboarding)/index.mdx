## Welcome to the Admin Panel

The admin panel is where HMM LMS content and members are managed. Everything students see on the platform — courses, tryouts, scholarships, announcements, events, job vacancies — is created and maintained here.

<Callout type="info" title="Who can access it?">
  Only accounts with the **Admin**, **BP**, or **Superadmin** role can open `/admin`. Other users are redirected to their dashboard. Your email must also be verified.
</Callout>

## Opening the Admin Panel

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in

Go to the sign-in page and log in with your admin account.

![Sign in](/images/docs/admin-guide/sign-in.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Go to the admin panel

Open `/admin` in your browser, or use the admin link from your profile menu in the top-right corner.

</Step>
<Step>

### Use the sidebar

The sidebar on the left lists every admin section, grouped by purpose. Click the **sidebar toggle** at the top to collapse or expand it.

![Admin dashboard with sidebar](/images/docs/admin-guide/dashboard.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Sidebar Map

| Group | Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | **Dashboard** | Platform summary, pending actions, recent activity |
| General | **Analytics** | Detailed charts and reports |
| General | **Forms** | Build forms and read responses |
| General | **Short Links** | Create short, trackable links and QR codes |
| Content Management | **Courses** | Courses, resources and members |
| Content Management | **Scholarships** | Scholarship listings |
| Content Management | **Tryouts** | Quizzes and exams |
| Content Management | **Announcements** | Global or course announcements |
| Content Management | **Events** | Events, RSVPs and attendance |
| Content Management | **M-Opportunity** | Job vacancies |
| User Management | **Users** | Members, roles and passwords |
| Machining Management | **Machining Batch** | Machining batch settings |
| Payload CMS | **Content Panel** | Public website content (opens `/admin-cms`) |
| System | **Database** | Browse and edit raw records |

<Callout type="warn" title="Some items depend on your role">
  Machining, BP profile and Superadmin pages are only available to the roles described in [Special Roles](/docs/admin/special-roles).
</Callout>

## Top Bar Tools

- **Search** — open the command search to jump to courses, announcements, scholarships, tryouts and events quickly.
- **Theme switch** — toggle light and dark mode.
- **Profile menu** — account options and sign out.
