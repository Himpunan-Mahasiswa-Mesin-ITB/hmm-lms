## What is the Galleries collection?

The `/gallery` page on the public HMM ITB website shows photo albums from HMM activities. Albums are stored in the **Galleries** collection of the **Content Panel** (Payload CMS) at `/admin-cms`.

Unlike news, events and achievements, an album has **no page of its own on the website**. Each album is a card with a cover image, description and tags. Clicking the card opens the album's **Google Drive folder**, where the full set of photos lives. The `/gallery` page lists the most recently published album first.

<Callout type="info" title="Who can post?">
  Only LMS accounts with the **Admin** or **Superadmin** role get a Content Panel account. Each time
  you sign in to the LMS, your account is copied to the CMS with the same email and password. Admins
  can create, edit and delete content.
</Callout>

## Before You Start

Upload the photos to a Google Drive folder first, then share the folder:

1. In Google Drive, right-click the folder and choose **Share** → **Share**.
2. Under **General access**, choose **Anyone with the link** with the **Viewer** role.
3. Click **Copy link**. You will paste this link into the CMS.

![Sharing a Google Drive folder](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-gallery-drive-share.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the sharing setting">
  If the folder is restricted, visitors who click the album will see a "You need access" page
  instead of the photos.
</Callout>

## Opening the Content Panel

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in to the CMS

Sign in to the LMS with your admin account, open **Content Panel** under the **Payload CMS** group in the admin sidebar (or go to `/admin-cms`), and log in with the **same email and password**. See [Posting News](/docs/admin/cms-news#opening-the-content-panel) for the full walkthrough.

![CMS dashboard](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-dashboard.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Find the Galleries collection

On the dashboard, open **Galleries** under **Content Pillar**. You can also open it from the menu button (☰) at the top-left.

![CMS Navbar](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-navbar.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Creating an Album

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start a new album

Open **Galleries** and click **Create New** next to the page title. The list shows every album with its description, publish date and status.

![Galleries list](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-gallery-list.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Drafts are saved automatically">
  The editor autosaves, so a draft is created as soon as you open **Create New**, before you type
  anything. If you change your mind, delete the draft (see [Editing or Deleting an
  Album](#editing-or-deleting-an-album)) so the Galleries list stays clean.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the title and cover image

On the **Content** tab:

![Album Content tab](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-gallery-content.png)

- **Album Title** (required): the name of the activity, for example _Malam Keakraban 2026_.
- **Album Image** (required): the cover image shown on the gallery card. Click **Create New** to upload a new image (or drag and drop a file onto the field), or click **Choose from existing** to pick one from the **Media** library.

When you upload a new image, fill in **Alt** (required). This is a short description of the image for screen readers and search engines. Click **Save** to upload it and attach it to the album.

![Album Content tab](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-photo-upload.png)

<Callout type="info" title="Upload only the cover">
  Only the cover image is uploaded to the CMS. The rest of the photos stay in Google Drive, so you
  don't need to upload the whole album here.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the Album Details

Open the **Album Details** tab:

- **Description** (required): a single line describing the album, shown on the gallery card.
- **Event Date** (optional): when the activity took place. If you leave it empty, it is set to the publish time.
- **Tags** (optional): click **Add Tag** for each keyword. The gallery card shows up to three tags.
- **Google Drive Link** (required): paste the folder link you copied in [Before You Start](#before-you-start).

![Album Details tab](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-gallery-details.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the sidebar fields

In the right sidebar:

- **Published Date**: the date shown on the gallery card. It also decides the order on `/gallery`. If you leave it empty, it is set when you publish.
- **Authors**: choose one or more CMS users as authors.
- **Publishing Status**: change it from **Draft** to **Published**.

![Album sidebar fields](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-gallery-sidebar.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish

Click **Publish changes** at the top-right. The status at the top of the page changes from **Draft** to **Published**, and the album appears on the `/gallery` page.

To publish later, click the arrow next to **Publish changes** and choose **Schedule Publish**, then pick a date and time.

![Publish and schedule](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-publish-menu.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Two settings must both be published">
  An album only appears on the website when **Publishing Status** is set to **Published** *and* you
  click **Publish changes**. Autosaved drafts are never shown publicly. If an album doesn't appear,
  check both settings.
</Callout>

</Step>
</Steps>

## Editing or Deleting an Album

- **Edit**: open the album from the Galleries list, make your changes, and click **Publish changes** again. Until you do, your edits are only a draft and the website keeps showing the previous version.
- **Unpublish**: set **Publishing Status** back to **Draft** and click **Publish changes**. The album disappears from `/gallery`.
- **Versions**: the **Versions** tab at the top of an album lists earlier saves. Open one to compare it or restore it.
- **Delete**: open the album, click the **⋮** menu at the top-right and choose **Delete**. You can also tick albums in the Galleries list and delete them in bulk. Deleting cannot be undone. The photos in Google Drive are not affected.

## Tips

<Callout type="idea" title="Making good albums">
  - Pick a clear, landscape cover image, since it is the only photo visitors see before opening the
  album. - Keep the description to one short sentence. Mention the activity and when it happened. -
  Open the Google Drive link in a private/incognito window before publishing to make sure it works
  for visitors who aren't signed in.
</Callout>
