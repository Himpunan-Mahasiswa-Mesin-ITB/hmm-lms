## Managing Scholarships

Open **Scholarships** in the sidebar (Content Management group). Each listing appears on the student **Scholarships** page.

![Scholarships list](/images/docs/admin-guide/scholarships.png)

## Creating a Scholarship

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Click the create button at the top of the Scholarships page.

![Create scholarship](/images/docs/admin-guide/scholarships-create.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the scholarship details

- **Title** and **Provider** (the organisation offering it).
- **Type** — **Internal** (run within the department/university) or **External**.
- **Description**, **Requirements** and **Benefits**.
- **Deadline** — the last day to apply.
- **Quota (Optional)** — number of recipients.
- **Link** — where students apply or read more.
- **Cover Image** — upload an image.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Submit the form. The scholarship is now visible to students.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Editing or Deleting

Click a scholarship in the list to edit it. After saving you will see *Scholarship updated successfully*. Use the delete option to remove it.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Keep deadlines up to date. Students use the deadline to decide what to apply for first.
</Callout>
