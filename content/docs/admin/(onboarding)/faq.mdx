## Access

### I get redirected to the dashboard when I open /admin

Your account does not have the **Admin**, **BP** or **Superadmin** role. Ask a Superadmin to update your role in [Users](/docs/admin/users).

### I am sent to a "not verified" page

Your email is not verified yet. Open the verification email and click the link, then sign in again.

### I cannot see Profiles or the Superadmin Area

Those pages are limited to BP and Superadmin roles. See [Special Roles](/docs/admin/special-roles).

## Content

### Who sees my announcement?

**Global** announcements are shown to everyone. **Course** announcements are shown only to members of that course.

### Why can't I upload images to an announcement?

Save the announcement first, then upload images.

### A student says they cannot see a new course or tryout

Check that the course is created with the right **type** and **scope**, and that the tryout is attached to the correct course.

### How do I share a long link on a poster?

Create a [Short Link](/docs/admin/short-links) and print its **QR Code**.

### How do I make an event with attendance?

Choose **Attendance Only** or **RSVP & Attendance** as the Event Mode. See [Events](/docs/admin/events).

## Users

### How do I reset a member's password?

Open **Users**, click the pencil (**Edit**) icon on the member's row, then press **Reset password** and confirm. The new password is shown once — share it securely.

### How do I make someone an admin?

Edit the user and change the **Role**. Only grant it to trusted members.

## Troubleshooting

### Analytics is slow

Analytics reads a lot of data. Choose a shorter date range.

### I deleted something by mistake

Deleting is permanent and there is no undo from the panel. Contact a developer immediately, as the database may have backups.
