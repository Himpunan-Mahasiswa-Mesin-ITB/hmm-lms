## Admin Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see in the admin panel. Open it any time by clicking **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar.

![Admin dashboard](/images/docs/admin-guide/dashboard.png)

## What You See

### Summary cards

Four cards at the top give a quick health check:

- **Total Users** — all registered members.
- **Active Courses** — courses currently available to students.
- **Today's Activity** — learning activity recorded today.
- **Pending Actions** — items that need your attention.

### Alerts

When something important needs review, a colored alert banner appears above the cards.

### Action Required

Lists the items that need attention right now. Click the arrow button on any row to jump straight to the page where you can resolve it. When nothing is pending you will see **All caught up!**.

### Upcoming Events

Events scheduled in the **next 7 days**, so you can confirm details before they start.

### Recent Activity

A feed of the latest actions across the platform, such as new members joining.

### Quick Actions

Shortcut buttons for common tasks: **Manage Users**, **Manage Courses**, **Manage Events**, **Manage Tryouts** and more. Scroll down to see them all.

## Quick Navigation

To see more detail, press **View Full Analytics** at the top-right of the dashboard. It opens the [Analytics](/docs/admin/analytics) page.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Use the search in the top bar to jump directly to a course, announcement, scholarship, tryout or event without going through the sidebar.
</Callout>
