## Managing Events

Open **Events** in the sidebar (Content Management group). Events show on the student **Events** and **Schedule** pages.

![Events list](/images/docs/admin-guide/events.png)

## Creating an Event

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Click **Create Event** on the Events page. A draft ("Untitled Event") is created and opens in the editor.

![Create event](/images/docs/admin-guide/events-create.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Basic Information

- **Title**, **Description** and **Location**.
- **Start / End Date & Time**, or tick **All day event**.
- **Hide Event Time** — show only the date to students.
- **Event Scope**: **Global**, **Machining**, **Course** (pick the course) or **Personal**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the Event Mode

| Mode | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic (No tracking)** | Just shown on the calendar |
| **RSVP Only** | Students reply whether they will come |
| **Attendance Only** | Presence is recorded at the event |
| **RSVP & Attendance** | Both |

Depending on the mode you can set:

- **RSVP Deadline** and **Require approval for RSVPs**.
- **Require approval for attendance**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Optional timeline

Switch on **Enable timeline** to add a schedule of time slots (for example `09:00` with a title and description) for the day.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Submit the form to publish the event.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Managing RSVPs and Attendance

Open an event from the list to see its detail page:

- **RSVPs** tab — each reply: **Confirmed**, **Attending with Notice**, or declined. When approval is required, review them under **Pending Approvals** and set the status to **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Pending**.
- **Check-ins** tab — attendance records, with status such as **Present**. Approve or reject where approval is required.
- Add **Notes** to any record for internal tracking.

## Editing or Deleting

Each event card shows its status (**Ongoing** / **Ended**), scope (**Global**), mode (e.g. **Basic**, **Rsvp And Attendance**), date, time, location and the number of RSVPs and check-ins. Use the **“…”** menu at the top-right of the card to edit or delete the event. Deleting an event also removes its RSVPs and attendance records.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Choose the simplest mode that fits. Use **RSVP & Attendance** for events where you need both a headcount before and a record after.
</Callout>
