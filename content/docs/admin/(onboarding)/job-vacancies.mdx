## M-Opportunity

**M-Opportunity** (sidebar → Content Management) manages the job vacancies students see on the student **Job Opportunities** page.

![Job vacancies](/images/docs/admin-guide/loker.png)

Use the search box (*Search job vacancies...*) to find a vacancy.

## Creating a Vacancy

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the dialog

Click the create button at the top of the page. The **Create Job Vacancy** dialog opens.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the details

- **Job Title** (e.g. *Frontend Developer*), **Company** and **Position/Location** (e.g. *Jakarta, Remote*).
- **Employment type** (e.g. Internship, Full-time) and **Seniority**.
- **Salary (display)** — free text such as *Competitive* or *IDR 8–12M / month*.
- **Overview** — description and requirements.
- **Eligibility** — who can apply.
- **Available Streams/Tracks**.
- **Timeline** — application deadline, interview process, etc.
- **Application Link** — the page where students apply.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Confirm. *Job vacancy created successfully* appears.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Editing, Closing and Deleting

- Use the **edit** action to open the **Edit Job Vacancy** dialog.
- Toggle the status to open or close a vacancy without deleting it.
- Use **delete** to remove it permanently.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Close a vacancy once its deadline passes rather than deleting it, so you keep the history.
</Callout>
