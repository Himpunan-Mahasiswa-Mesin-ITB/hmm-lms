## Managing Tryouts

Tryouts are timed quizzes attached to a course. Open **Tryouts** in the sidebar (Content Management group).

![Tryouts list](/images/docs/admin-guide/tryouts.png)

## Creating a Tryout

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Click **Create Tryout** on the Tryouts page. A draft ("Untitled Tryout") is created and opens in the editor.

![Create tryout](/images/docs/admin-guide/tryouts-create.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Basic Information

- **Title** and **Description (Optional)**.
- **Course** — pick the course the tryout belongs to.
- **Duration** — in minutes (e.g. `60`).
- **Multiple Attempt** — allow students to retake the tryout.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add questions

Click to add a question, then fill in:

- **Question Type**: multiple choice (single answer), multiple choice (multiple answers), short answer or long answer.
- **Question** text, plus optional **Question Images**.
- **Answer Options** — mark the correct one(s). Options can have images too.
- **Points** for the question.
- **Explanation** — shown after the student finishes. You can also write an **Overall Explanation (Optional)** with images.

You can reorder with the shuffle control and remove a question or option at any time.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Save the tryout to publish it to the course.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing Results

Open a tryout from the list to see:

- **Statistics** — average score and overall performance.
- **Recent Attempts** — student, score, duration, start time and status.

Use this to find questions most students get wrong.

<Callout type="warn" title="Editing after attempts">
  Changing questions or points after students have attempted the tryout can make earlier scores inconsistent with the new version.
</Callout>
