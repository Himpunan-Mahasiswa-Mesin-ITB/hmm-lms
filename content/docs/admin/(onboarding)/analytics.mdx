## Analytics & Reports

Open **Analytics** from the sidebar (General group) to see detailed charts about how the platform is used.

![Analytics page](/images/docs/admin-guide/analytics.png)

## Tabs

The page is split into tabs: **Overview**, **Users**, **Courses**, **Tryouts**, **Resources**, **Events** and **Health**. **Overview** shows headline cards (**Total Users**, **Active Courses**, **Platform Health**, **Avg Session**), totals for events, scholarships and resources, and the **Platform Activity Trends** chart. The other tabs hold the detailed reports below.

## Report Sections

### Users

- **User Registration Trend** and **User Growth Rate** — how fast the membership grows.
- **Role Distribution**, **Faculty Distribution** and **Program Distribution** — who your members are.
- **Active Users Breakdown** — **Daily Active Users (DAU)**, **Monthly Active Users (MAU)** and the **DAU/MAU ratio** (platform stickiness).
- **Retention Rate** — the share of users who come back.

### Learning

- **Learning Activity** and **Platform Activity Trends** over time.
- **Course Performance Ranking** and **Course Satisfaction** (average rating and testimonials).
- **Most Accessed Resources** — views and downloads per resource.
- **Leaderboard - Top Performers** and **Most Active Users**.

### Tryouts

- **Tryout Attempts Over Time**.
- **Top Performing Tryouts** and **Tryout Completion** rate.

### Events & Forms

- **Event Attendance**, **Event Performance**, **RSVP Status Breakdown** and **Attendance Status Breakdown**.
- **Form Submissions** per form.

## Changing the Time Range

By default the page shows the **last 30 days**; the subtitle under the title shows the exact range. Use the **date range picker** at the top-right of the page to choose a different period (presets: Last 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, 2 weeks, month, 3 months, or a **Custom range**). All charts reload for the new range, and comparison cards show the change against the previous period.

## Exporting

Press the **Export** button next to the date range picker to download the analytics for the selected period.

<Callout type="info" title="Loading time">
  Analytics reads a lot of data, so it may show a loading screen for a few seconds the first time you open it.
</Callout>
