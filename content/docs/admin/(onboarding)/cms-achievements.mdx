## What is the Achievements collection?

Achievements on the public HMM ITB website (competition wins, awards and other recognitions earned by members) are stored in the **Achievements** collection of the **Content Panel** (Payload CMS) at `/admin-cms`. Each published achievement appears on the `/achievements` page and gets its own page at `/achievements/<slug>`. The `/achievements` page lists the most recent award first.

<Callout type="info" title="Who can post?">
  Only LMS accounts with the **Admin** or **Superadmin** role get a Content Panel account. Each time
  you sign in to the LMS, your account is copied to the CMS with the same email and password. Admins
  can create, edit and delete content.
</Callout>

## Opening the Content Panel

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in to the CMS

Sign in to the LMS with your admin account, open **Content Panel** under the **Payload CMS** group in the admin sidebar (or go to `/admin-cms`), and log in with the **same email and password**. See [Posting News](/docs/admin/cms-news#opening-the-content-panel) for the full walkthrough.

![CMS dashboard](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-dashboard.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Find the Achievements collection

On the dashboard, open **Achievements** under **Content Pillar**. You can also open it from the menu button (☰) at the top-left.

![CMS Navbar](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-navbar.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Creating an Achievement

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start a new achievement

Open **Achievements** and click **Create New** next to the page title. The list shows every achievement with its competition, award level and date.

![Achievements list](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-achievements-list.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Drafts are saved automatically">
  The editor autosaves, so a draft is created as soon as you open **Create New**, before you type
  anything. If you change your mind, delete the draft (see [Editing or Deleting an
  Achievement](#editing-or-deleting-an-achievement)) so the Achievements list stays clean.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the title and story

At the top of the page and on the **Content** tab:

- **Achievement Title** (required): the headline, for example _1st Place at Indonesian Rocket Competition 2026_. The **Slug** in the right sidebar is generated from it automatically.
- **Full Story & Project Description** (required): the story behind the award: the competition, the team's project, and how they won. Use the toolbar to add headings, lists, alignment, bold/italic, links and more.

![Achievement editor](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-achievements-editor.png)

Type `/` on an empty line to open the command menu. Besides headings and lists, you can insert three blocks:

| Block           | Use it for                                                                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Banner**      | A highlighted notice inside the story                                                         |
| **Code**        | A formatted code snippet with a copy button                                                   |
| **Media Block** | An image from the Media library placed inside the story, for example a photo of the prototype |

![Slash command menu](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-slash-menu.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a trophy or team photo

Under **Trophy / Team Photo** you can:

- Click **Create New** to upload a new image (or drag and drop a file onto the field), or
- Click **Choose from existing** to pick an image already in the **Media** library.

When you upload a new image, fill in **Alt** (required). This is a short description of the image for screen readers and search engines. Click **Save** to upload it and attach it to the achievement.

![Uploading a team photo](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-photo-upload.png)

<Callout type="info" title="Where images are stored">
  Uploaded images go to the shared **Media** collection, which is stored on DigitalOcean Spaces. You
  can reuse them in other posts.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the Achievement Details

Open the **Achievement Details** tab:

- **Competition / Contest Name** (required): for example _Shell Eco-marathon_, _KMHE_ or _KTI Nasional_.
- **Award Level** (required): choose _1st Place_, _2nd Place_, _3rd Place_, _Honorable Mention_, _Finalist_, _Special Award_ or _Other_.
- **Specify Award Level**: appears only when you choose **Other**. Type the award name, for example _Best Design_. This text is shown on the website instead of "Other".
- **Date Awarded** (required): when the award was received. It decides the order on `/achievements`.
- **Team Members & NIMs** (optional): click the add button below the field for each winner and fill in **Student Name** (required) and **NIM**.
- **Tags** (optional): click **Add Tag** for each keyword.

![Achievement Details tab](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-achievements-details.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Optimise for search (optional)

The **SEO** tab controls how the achievement looks in Google results and link previews:

- **Title**: 50–60 characters. Click **Auto-generate** to use _Achievement Title | HMM ITB_.
- **Meta Image**: the image shown when the link is shared. Usually the same as the trophy or team photo.
- **Description**: 100–150 characters.

The checklist at the top (_0/3 checks are passing_) and the **Preview** at the bottom show what is still missing.

![SEO tab](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-seo.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the sidebar fields

In the right sidebar:

- **Published At**: the date the achievement was posted. If you leave it empty, it is set when you publish.
- **Authors**: choose one or more CMS users as authors.
- **Publishing Status**: change it from **Draft** to **Published**.
- **Slug**: the URL of the achievement (`/achievements/<slug>`). It follows the title. Click **Unlock** to edit it manually.

![Achievement sidebar fields](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-achievements-sidebar.png)

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish

Click **Publish changes** at the top-right. The status at the top of the page changes from **Draft** to **Published**, and the achievement appears on the `/achievements` page.

To publish later, click the arrow next to **Publish changes** and choose **Schedule Publish**, then pick a date and time.

![Publish and schedule](/images/docs/admin-guide/cms-publish-menu.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Two settings must both be published">
  An achievement only appears on the website when **Publishing Status** is set to **Published**
  *and* you click **Publish changes**. Autosaved drafts are never shown publicly. If an achievement
  doesn't appear, check both settings.
</Callout>

</Step>
</Steps>

## Editing or Deleting an Achievement

- **Edit**: open the achievement from the Achievements list, make your changes, and click **Publish changes** again. Until you do, your edits are only a draft and the live page keeps showing the previous version.
- **Unpublish**: set **Publishing Status** back to **Draft** and click **Publish changes**. The achievement disappears from `/achievements`.
- **Versions**: the **Versions** tab at the top of an achievement lists earlier saves. Open one to compare it or restore it.
- **Delete**: open the achievement, click the **⋮** menu at the top-right and choose **Delete**. You can also tick achievements in the Achievements list and delete them in bulk. Deleting cannot be undone.

## Tips

<Callout type="idea" title="Writing good achievement posts">
  - Put the award and the competition in the title, for example *2nd Place at KMHE 2026*. The title
  also becomes the URL. - Always add a trophy or team photo, because the achievement cards use it. -
  Double-check team members' names and NIMs with the team before publishing. - Use the eye icon next
  to **Publish changes** to open **Live Preview** and check the page as readers will see it before
  you publish.
</Callout>
