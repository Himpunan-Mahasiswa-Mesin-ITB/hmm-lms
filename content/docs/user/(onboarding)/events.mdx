## The Events Page

Select **Events** on the sidebar. The events page displays events in three tabs:

- **All Events**: every event you can see.
- **My Events**: your personal events.
- **Course Events**: events from your enrolled courses.

Click an event to see its details: date and time, location, description, timeline, who created it, and the RSVP deadline.

## RSVP

If the event uses RSVP, choose whether you will attend (**Will Attend** / **Won't Attend**) before the RSVP deadline.

## Checking In

If the event takes attendance, click **Check In** to mark yourself as present.

<Callout type="info">
  Check-in opens **one hour before** the event starts. The button will not be available before then.
</Callout>

## From the Dashboard

You can also RSVP and check in from the calendar on the [Dashboard](/docs/user/dashboard#using-the-calendar). To see all events on a full calendar, use the [Schedule](/docs/user/schedule) page.
