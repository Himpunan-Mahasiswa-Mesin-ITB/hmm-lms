## Introduction

This guide covers every feature you can reach from the user dashboard of hmmitb.com.

<Callout>
  You must be signed in with a verified account to use the dashboard. If your account has not been
  verified yet, you will be redirected to a verification notice page after signing in.
</Callout>

## Getting Around

When you open the hmmitb.com website, you will see a display like this:

![Image 4](/images/docs/user-guide/image4.png)

The header at the top of every page contains:

- **Sidebar toggle** (the panel icon at the top left). Press it to show or hide the sidebar. The sidebar remembers whether you left it open or closed.
- **Breadcrumb**, which shows the page you are currently on (for example, _Dashboard_ or _Courses / Getaran Mekanik_).
- **Search** (`Ctrl K`, or `⌘ K` on Mac). Opens a quick search window where you can jump to any page, or search directly for a course, tryout, announcement, event, or scholarship by name. Use the arrow keys to move between results, `Enter` to open one, and `Esc` to close the window.
- **Theme switch** (the sun/moon toggle at the top right). Switches the website between light and dark mode.

![Image 23](/images/docs/user-guide/image23.png)

The sidebar lists all the pages you can access, grouped into sections:

| Section     | Pages                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General     | [Dashboard](/docs/user/dashboard), [Hall of Fame](/docs/user/hall-of-fame), [Schedule](/docs/user/schedule)                                                                             |
| Academics   | [Courses](/docs/user/courses), [Tryouts](/docs/user/tryouts), [Scholarships](/docs/user/scholarships)                                                                                   |
| Himpunan    | [Events](/docs/user/events), [Announcements](/docs/user/announcements), [M-Opportunity](/docs/user/m-opportunity), [Hotline](/docs/user/hotline), [Competition](/docs/user/competition) |
| Preferences | [Settings](/docs/user/settings)                                                                                                                                                         |

At the bottom of the sidebar is your **profile menu**, showing your name and role. Click it to open your [Profile](/docs/user/profile) page or to **Sign Out**.
