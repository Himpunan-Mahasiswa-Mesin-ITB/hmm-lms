## Introduction

This guide covers every feature you can reach from the user dashboard of hmmitb.com. The Admin Panel and Machining pages are only visible to admins and are not covered here.

<Callout>
  You must be signed in with a verified account to use the dashboard. If your account has not been verified yet, you will be redirected to a verification notice page after signing in.
</Callout>

### Getting Around

When you open the hmmitb.com website, you will see a display like this:

![Image 4](/images/docs/user-guide/image4.png)

The header at the top of every page contains:

- **Sidebar toggle** (the panel icon at the top left). Press it to show or hide the sidebar. The sidebar remembers whether you left it open or closed.
- **Breadcrumb**, which shows the page you are currently on (for example, _Dashboard_ or _Courses / Getaran Mekanik_).
- **Search** (`Ctrl K`, or `⌘ K` on Mac). Opens a quick search window where you can jump to any page, or search directly for a course, tryout, announcement, event, or scholarship by name. Use the arrow keys to move between results, `Enter` to open one, and `Esc` to close the window.
- **Theme switch** (the sun/moon toggle at the top right). Switches the website between light and dark mode.

![Image 23](/images/docs/user-guide/image23.png)

The sidebar lists all the pages you can access, grouped into sections:

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| General | Dashboard, Hall of Fame, Schedule |
| Academics | Courses, Tryouts, Scholarships |
| Himpunan | Events, Announcements, M-Opportunity, Hotline, Competition |
| Preferences | Settings |

At the bottom of the sidebar is your **profile menu**, showing your name and role. Click it to open your **Profile** page or to **Sign Out**.

## General

### Dashboard

![Image 6](/images/docs/user-guide/image6.png)

The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It summarizes your learning activity and what's coming up. It contains:

1. **Weekly Learning Focus**. Shortcuts to **Continue Learning** (opens Courses) and **Upcoming Events** (opens Events).
2. **Profile Progress**. If you have been assigned one or more progress profiles, this card shows your overall progress percentage. Click a group to expand it and see the progress of each profile in it, along with when it was last updated.
3. **Continue Your Courses**. Up to four of your enrolled courses, each showing the class code and its number of videos and materials. Click a course to open it, or click **View all** to go to the Courses page. If you haven't enrolled in any course yet, click **Browse Courses** to find one.
4. **Activity Minutes**. A bar chart of how many minutes you spent learning on the website on each of the last seven days.
5. **Hall of Fame**. The top three learners of the week. Click **View full ranking** to open the Hall of Fame page.
6. **Calendar**. A small calendar where days with events or forms are marked. Select a date to see that day's events and forms:
   - Click an event to see its time, location, and number of attendees. From there you can **RSVP** (**Will Attend** / **Won't Attend**) before the RSVP deadline, **Check In** to record your attendance (available from one hour before the event starts), or click **View Details** to open the event page.
   - Click a form to read its description and open it, as long as the form is currently open.

### Hall of Fame

![Image 5](/images/docs/user-guide/image5.png)

The Hall of Fame ranks the users who spent the most time learning on hmmitb.com this week. The top three are shown on a podium, followed by the rest of the top 20 along with their learning minutes for the week. The ranking resets every week.

At the start of each new week, a **Weekly Hall of Fame** pop-up shows the current top three learners when you open the website. Click **Awesome, let's learn** to close it. It will not appear again until the next week.

### Schedule

The schedule page contains a calendar with upcoming activities, such as events held by HMM ITB, exam schedules, and course activities.

1. Select **Schedule** on the sidebar.
2. Use the **Filter** button to choose which events are shown: **Personal** (only visible to you), **Course** (from courses you are enrolled in), and **Global** (HMM ITB-wide events).
3. Change the calendar view to show the events of a month, a week, a day, or a list (agenda).
4. Click an event on the calendar to see its details.

Only admins can create, edit, and delete events on the schedule.

## Academics

![Image 18](/images/docs/user-guide/image18.png)

The academics section contains information related to academic activities, such as study materials, tryouts for exams, and a variety of scholarships.

### Courses

Courses is a centralized repository of academic information, grouped by the subjects taught in the mechanical engineering curriculum.

![Image 8](/images/docs/user-guide/image8.png)

![Image 3](/images/docs/user-guide/image3.png)

The courses page has two tabs: **My Courses** (courses you are enrolled in) and **All Courses** (every available course).

To access information in a course, such as teaching materials, exam questions, and tutorial videos, you must first enroll in the course:

1. Select **Courses** on the sidebar.
2. Open the **All Courses** tab to see all available courses.
3. Choose the course for the subject you want to enroll in. A course preview shows its syllabus, the number of enrolled students, and what you will get access to.
4. Click **Enroll in Course** to access all the information related to that subject.

Inside an enrolled course, the content is divided into tabs:

- **Materials**: syllabus, notes, presentations and slides, e-books, and other resources.
- **Videos**: learning videos for the course.
- **Problems**: problem sets and past exams.
- **Tryouts**: practice tryouts for the course.

The course page also shows the course's **Recent Announcements**. To leave a course, use the **Unenroll** button on the course page and confirm.

Time you spend studying course content is counted toward your weekly learning minutes, which appear in your Activity Minutes chart, your profile, and the Hall of Fame.

### Tryouts

Tryouts support your learning by simulating questions that may appear in college exams.

![Image 19](/images/docs/user-guide/image19.png)

![Image 12](/images/docs/user-guide/image12.png)

![Image 10](/images/docs/user-guide/image10.png)

![Image 7](/images/docs/user-guide/image7.png)

The tryouts page groups tryouts by course. To access a course's tryouts, enroll in the course first. Then:

1. Select **Tryouts** on the sidebar.
2. Choose the tryout you want to attempt. For example, to prepare for the 3rd partial exam of Heat Transfer, choose _Tryout UP3 Perpan_.
3. The tryout details page shows the question overview, the time limit, whether you can attempt it more than once, and whether you can view the correct answers afterwards. Click **Start New Attempt**. The timer starts immediately.
4. The questions are shown along with their answer choices or an answer box.
5. Use the **Navigator** to jump between questions and see which ones you have answered. Your answers are saved as you go.
6. Finish the tryout before the time limit runs out. When time is up, the tryout is submitted automatically.
7. Click **Submit** once you have answered all questions, then confirm. Submitting cannot be undone.
8. After submitting, the results page shows your score, the number of questions answered, and the time taken. If the tryout allows it, the **Question Review** shows the correct answer and an explanation for each question.

If you leave in the middle of an attempt, you can continue it from the tryout details page, which marks it as **In Progress**.

### Scholarships

Scholarships displays information about scholarships you can apply for to support your studies.

![Image 2](/images/docs/user-guide/image2.png)

![Image 16](/images/docs/user-guide/image16.png)

To view or apply for a scholarship:

1. Select **Scholarships** on the sidebar.
2. The scholarships page lists the currently available scholarships, with their type, deadline, and quota.
3. Click **Details** to see more complete information about a scholarship.
4. The scholarship page lists its benefits, requirements for applicants, and any additional resources.
5. Click **Apply Now** to register through the scholarship provider's website.

## Himpunan

Himpunan contains information related to HMM ITB, including events, announcements, M-Opportunity, Hotline, and competitions.

![Image 22](/images/docs/user-guide/image22.png)

### Events

![Image 13](/images/docs/user-guide/image13.png)

![Image 14](/images/docs/user-guide/image14.png)

To view events:

1. Select **Events** on the sidebar.
2. The events page displays events in three tabs: **All Events**, **My Events** (your personal events), and **Course Events** (events from your enrolled courses).
3. Click an event to see its details: date and time, location, description, timeline, who created it, and the RSVP deadline.
4. If the event uses RSVP, choose whether you will attend before the RSVP deadline.
5. If the event takes attendance, click **Check In** to mark yourself as present. Check-in opens one hour before the event starts.

You can also RSVP and check in from the calendar on the dashboard.

### Announcements

![Image 15](/images/docs/user-guide/image15.png)

To view announcements:

1. Select **Announcements** on the sidebar.
2. You will see the latest announcements about HMM ITB activities. Click **See more** to open an announcement in full.
3. Reply to an announcement by clicking **Reply**, writing your message, and sending it. All replies are shown on the announcement's page.

### M-Opportunity

![Image 9](/images/docs/user-guide/image9.png)

M-Opportunity lists job and internship openings shared with HMM ITB members.

1. Select **M-Opportunity** on the sidebar.
2. Use **Search & filters** to find a role:
   - Search by job title or company.
   - Filter by **location** (e.g. Bandung, Jakarta), **seniority** (intern, junior, mid, senior), and **employment type** (intern, part-time, full-time, contract).
   - Filter by the related engineering **stream**.
3. Click an opening to see its job information: company, position, level, type, salary, required streams, and when it was posted.
4. Click **Apply Now** to apply through the link provided.

### Hotline

![Image 25](/images/docs/user-guide/image25.png)

Hotline is where you can submit reports and requests to HMM ITB.

1. Select **Hotline** on the sidebar.
2. You will see the active hotline forms, each with information about its reporting flow.
3. To submit a report, click **Open Form** on the topic you want to report and fill it in.

### Competition

Competition lists registration forms for competitions that are currently open.

1. Select **Competition** on the sidebar.
2. You will see the active competition forms, each with a short description and when it was last updated.
3. Click **Open Form** to register or submit your entry.
4. Click **Load More** at the bottom of the page to see older competitions.

## Preferences

Preferences lets you adjust the website's user interface (UI) to your liking.

![Image 26](/images/docs/user-guide/image26.png)

### Settings

![Image 11](/images/docs/user-guide/image11.png)

![Image 24](/images/docs/user-guide/image24.png)

To change your display settings:

1. Select **Settings** on the sidebar.
2. **Theme**. Choose a color theme preset (Default, Zinc, Rose, Teal, Vivid Pop, Aurora Mix) or **Custom**. With Custom, set a base color or randomize it, fine-tune individual colors for both light and dark mode, and click **Reset custom palette** to start over.
3. **Typography**. Set the font family and font size of the website, with a preview of the result.
4. **Layout Spacing**. Adjust the overall spacing and density of the interface.
5. **Accessibility**. Turn on **Contrast** for stronger borders and focus colors, set **Motion** to control animations, underline links to make them easier to spot, and enable a thicker focus outline for keyboard navigation. These preferences are saved in your current browser only.
6. **Language**. A language setting is coming soon, to make the website easier to use for those who are not fluent in Indonesian.
7. To return the UI to its default appearance, click **Reset** at the bottom of the page.

## Account

### Profile

Open your profile by clicking your name at the bottom of the sidebar and selecting **Profile**.

The profile page shows your personal information (name, NIM, email, faculty, program, and position) along with your learning statistics: active courses, tryout attempts, and weekly learning minutes.

To edit your profile:

1. Click **Edit Profile** on the profile page.
2. Change your profile photo and cover photo (you can crop the image before saving).
3. Update your full name and position. Your NIM, email, faculty, and program cannot be changed.
4. To change your password, enter your current password, then your new password twice.
5. Save your changes.

### Signing Out

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Sign Out**.
