Competition page lists registration forms for competitions that are currently open, submitting member's competition records, and achievement/accomplishments records

![Image 4](/images/docs/user-guide/competition.png)

## Registering for a Competition

1. Select **Competition** on the sidebar.
2. You will see the active competition type forms, each with a short description and when it was last updated.
3. Click **Open Form** to register or submit your entry.
4. Click **Load More** at the bottom of the page to see older competitions.
