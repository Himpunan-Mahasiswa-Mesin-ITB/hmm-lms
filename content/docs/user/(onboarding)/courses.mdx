The **Academics** section of the sidebar contains information related to academic activities, such as study materials, [tryouts](/docs/user/tryouts) for exams, and a variety of [scholarships](/docs/user/scholarships).

Courses is a centralized repository of academic information, grouped by the subjects taught in the mechanical engineering curriculum.

## The Courses Page

The courses page has two tabs:

- **My Courses**: courses you are enrolled in.
- **All Courses**: every available course.

## Enrolling in a Course

To access information in a course, such as teaching materials, exam questions, and tutorial videos, you must first enroll in the course:

1. Select **Courses** on the sidebar.
2. Open the **All Courses** tab to see all available courses.
3. Choose the course for the subject you want to enroll in. The course page shows its lecturers and description, and a course preview shows its syllabus, the number of enrolled students, and what you will get access to.
4. Click **Enroll in Course** to access all the information related to that subject.

## Inside a Course

Inside an enrolled course, the content is divided into tabs:

| Tab           | Contents                                                             |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Materials** | Syllabus, notes, presentations and slides, e-books, and other resources |
| **Videos**    | Learning videos for the course                                       |
| **Problems**  | Problem sets and past exams                                          |
| **Tryouts**   | Practice [tryouts](/docs/user/tryouts) for the course                |

The course page also shows the course's **Recent Announcements**.

## Leaving a Course

To leave a course, use the **Unenroll** button on the course page and confirm.

<Callout type="warn">
  After unenrolling, you lose access to the course's materials, videos, problems, and tryouts until you enroll again. Its course events will also stop appearing on your [Schedule](/docs/user/schedule).
</Callout>

## Learning Minutes

Time you spend studying course content is counted toward your weekly learning minutes, which appear in your **Activity Minutes** chart on the [Dashboard](/docs/user/dashboard), your [Profile](/docs/user/profile), and the [Hall of Fame](/docs/user/hall-of-fame).
