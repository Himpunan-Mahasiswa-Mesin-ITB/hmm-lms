## Course Management

The Courses section is your gateway to all learning materials and educational content on the HMM LMS platform. Here you can browse available courses, manage your enrollments, and access your learning resources.

<Callout type="info" title="Quick Access">
  You can access courses from the main sidebar navigation or directly from your dashboard.
</Callout>

## Course Browsing

### 📚 **All Courses Tab**

Browse the complete catalog of available courses:

- **Course Discovery**: Explore all courses offered on the platform
- **Course Information**: View titles, class codes, and enrollment counts
- **Tryout Availability**: See which courses include assessments
- **Visual Cards**: Each course displays with relevant imagery and key details

### 🎯 **My Courses Tab**

View and manage your personal course enrollments:

- **Enrolled Courses**: Quick access to courses you're currently taking
- **Progress Tracking**: See your enrollment status and activity
- **Material Counts**: View available videos and learning materials
- **Direct Access**: One-click access to course content

## Course Information

Each course card displays essential information:

### 📋 **Course Details**

- **Title**: Full course name and description
- **Class Code**: Unique identifier for the course
- **Type**: Course category or classification
- **Enrollment Status**: Whether you're enrolled or not

### 📊 **Course Statistics**

- **Member Count**: Number of students enrolled (for All Courses)
- **Video Count**: Available video lessons
- **Material Count**: Downloadable resources and documents
- **Tryout Count**: Available assessments and quizzes

## Enrolling in Courses

### 🎓 **How to Enroll**

1. Navigate to the **All Courses** tab
2. Browse through available courses
3. Click on a course card to view details
4. Use the enrollment button to join the course
5. The course will appear in your **My Courses** tab

### ✅ **Enrollment Benefits**

Once enrolled, you gain access to:

- Full course content and materials
- Video lessons and tutorials
- Downloadable resources
- Course tryouts and assessments
- Progress tracking and analytics

## Course Content Access

### 📖 **Accessing Course Materials**

After enrolling, access course content through:

- **My Courses Tab**: Direct link to enrolled courses
- **Dashboard**: Quick access to active courses
- **Course Cards**: Click on any enrolled course to enter

### 🎬 **Learning Resources**

Inside each course, you'll find:

- **Video Lessons**: Structured video content for learning
- **Attachments**: Downloadable materials and resources
- **Tryouts**: Assessments to test your knowledge
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your completion status

## Course Features

### 📈 **Progress Tracking**

- **Completion Status**: Track which lessons you've completed
- **Learning Analytics**: View your time spent on each course
- **Achievement Badges**: Earn recognition for milestones
- **Streak Maintenance**: Keep your learning momentum going

### 🔔 **Course Updates**

- **New Content**: Notifications when new materials are added
- **Announcements**: Course-specific news and updates
- **Deadline Reminders**: Alerts for important dates
- **Instructor Messages**: Direct communication from course instructors

## Course Navigation

### 🧭 **Within a Course**

When you enter a course, you can:

- **Browse Content**: Navigate through lessons and materials
- **Track Progress**: See your completion percentage
- **Access Tryouts**: Take available assessments
- **View Resources**: Download supplementary materials

### 📱 **Mobile Access**

Courses are fully accessible on mobile devices:

- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for tablets and smartphones
- **Touch Navigation**: Easy-to-use mobile interface
- **Offline Access**: Download materials for offline learning (when available)

## Course Management Tips

### 🎯 **Best Practices**

1. **Start with Dashboard**: Use your dashboard to quickly resume learning
2. **Set Goals**: Establish weekly learning targets for each course
3. **Track Progress**: Regularly check your completion status
4. **Stay Organized**: Focus on one course at a time for better retention

### 📊 **Time Management**

- **Schedule Learning**: Block dedicated time for each course
- **Prioritize**: Focus on courses with upcoming deadlines
- **Balance**: Distribute your time across multiple enrollments
- **Review**: Regularly revisit completed courses for reinforcement

## Troubleshooting

### Course Access Issues

- **Enrollment Problems**: Contact your instructor or administrator
- **Content Not Loading**: Check your internet connection
- **Missing Materials**: Verify with the course instructor

### Progress Tracking

- **Progress Not Updating**: Refresh the page or check your connection
- **Completion Status Issues**: Contact support if progress seems incorrect
- **Sync Problems**: Ensure you're logged into your account
