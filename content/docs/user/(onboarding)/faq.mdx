## Account

### I am sent to an "Email Not Verified" page after signing in

Your account has not been verified yet. Open the verification email sent to your inbox and click the link, then sign in again. If you can't find the email, click **Resend Verification Email** on that page.

### I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**, enter your email, and follow the password reset link sent to your inbox.

### How do I change my password?

Open your [Profile](/docs/user/profile#changing-your-password), click **Edit Profile**, enter your current password, then your new password twice.

### Can I change my NIM, email, faculty, or program?

No. Only your photos, full name, position, and password can be changed from your [Profile](/docs/user/profile).

## Courses and Tryouts

### I can't open a course's materials or tryouts

You need to enroll in the course first. Open **Courses**, go to the **All Courses** tab, choose the course, and click **Enroll in Course**. See [Courses](/docs/user/courses#enrolling-in-a-course).

### What happens if the time runs out during a tryout?

The tryout is submitted automatically with the answers you have saved so far. See [Tryouts](/docs/user/tryouts).

### I closed the page in the middle of a tryout

Open the tryout again. Your attempt is marked **In Progress** on the tryout details page, and you can continue it from there. The timer keeps running while you are away.

### Can I retake a tryout or see the correct answers?

It depends on the tryout. The tryout details page shows whether you can attempt it more than once and whether you can view the correct answers afterwards.

## Learning Minutes and Hall of Fame

### How are my learning minutes counted?

Time you spend studying course content is counted toward your weekly learning minutes. They appear in your **Activity Minutes** chart, your profile, and the [Hall of Fame](/docs/user/hall-of-fame).

### When does the Hall of Fame reset?

Every week. It shows the top 20 learners of the current week.

## Events and Schedule

### Why can't I check in to an event yet?

Check-in opens one hour before the event starts, and only for events that take attendance. See [Events](/docs/user/events#checking-in).

### What's the difference between Personal, Course, and Global events?

**Personal** events are only visible to you, **Course** events come from courses you are enrolled in, and **Global** events are HMM ITB-wide. Use the **Filter** button on the [Schedule](/docs/user/schedule) to choose which ones are shown.

### Can I add my own events to the schedule?

No. Only admins can create, edit, and delete events on the schedule.

## Display

### My accessibility settings disappeared

Accessibility preferences are saved in your current browser only. Set them again in [Settings](/docs/user/settings#accessibility) when you use a different browser or device.

### How do I undo my theme changes?

Click **Reset** at the bottom of the [Settings](/docs/user/settings#resetting) page to return to the default appearance, or **Reset custom palette** to clear only your custom colors.
