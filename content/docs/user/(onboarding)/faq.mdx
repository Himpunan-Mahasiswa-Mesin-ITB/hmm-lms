## Getting Started

### How do I create an account on HMM LMS?

Account creation is typically handled through your institution. Contact your administrator or check your institutional email for account setup instructions. If you're having trouble accessing your account, use the hotline forms to request assistance.

### What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in page to reset your password. You'll receive an email with instructions to create a new password. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support through hotline forms.

### How do I access the platform for the first time?

After receiving your account credentials, visit the platform URL and sign in using your email and password. You'll be prompted to complete your profile setup, including adding your academic information and personalizing your settings.

## Courses & Learning

### How do I enroll in a course?

Browse the "All Courses" tab to find available courses. Click on a course card to view details, then use the enrollment button to join. Once enrolled, the course will appear in your "My Courses" tab and on your dashboard.

### Can I enroll in multiple courses at once?

Yes, you can enroll in multiple courses simultaneously. There's typically no limit on the number of courses you can join, but we recommend balancing your workload to ensure quality learning.

### How do I access course materials?

After enrolling, click on the course from your "My Courses" tab or dashboard. Within the course, you'll find video lessons, reading materials, downloadable resources, and other learning content organized by modules or sections.

### What if course content isn't loading properly?

First, check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. If issues persist, try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser. For persistent problems, use hotline forms to report technical issues.

### Can I download course materials for offline use?

Many course materials, including documents and some videos, can be downloaded for offline study. Look for download buttons on individual materials. Availability depends on the instructor's settings and content type.

## Tryouts & Assessments

### How do I access tryouts for my courses?

Tryouts can be accessed through the dedicated Tryouts page, which shows all available assessments from your enrolled courses. You can also access tryouts directly within individual course pages.

### What happens if I run out of time during a timed tryout?

If the timer expires, your answers will be automatically submitted. Make sure to manage your time effectively during timed assessments. Some tryouts may not have time limits - check the details before starting.

### Can I retake a tryout if I'm not satisfied with my score?

This depends on the tryout settings. Some tryouts allow multiple attempts while others permit only a single attempt. Check the tryout details for retry policies. Your best score is typically recorded when multiple attempts are allowed.

### How are tryout scores calculated?

Tryout scores are calculated based on the number of correct answers and the scoring weight of each question. You'll receive a detailed breakdown of your performance, including overall score and performance by question type or topic.

### What if I encounter technical issues during a tryout?

If you experience technical problems during an assessment, document the issue (take screenshots if possible) and immediately report it through hotline forms. Do not wait until after submission - report issues as soon as they occur.

## Dashboard & Analytics

### What information does my dashboard show?

Your dashboard displays enrolled courses, learning progress, upcoming events, weekly learning statistics, Hall of Fame rankings, and a calendar view. It's designed to give you a comprehensive overview of your learning activity at a glance.

### How is my learning time calculated for the Hall of Fame?

Learning time is calculated based on time spent actively engaged with course content, including watching videos, reading materials, and completing activities. Passive time doesn't count - the system detects active engagement.

### How often do Hall of Fame rankings update?

Hall of Fame rankings are calculated weekly. The leaderboard shows top performers for the current week, with rankings resetting at the beginning of each new week for fair competition.

### Can I customize what appears on my dashboard?

While the dashboard layout is standardized, you can influence what appears by enrolling in courses, participating in events, and engaging with content. Your settings and theme choices also affect the visual appearance.

## Profile & Settings

### How do I update my profile information?

Go to your Profile page and click the "Edit Profile" button. You can update your name, email, academic information (faculty, program, position), profile picture, and cover image. Changes are saved immediately.

### What theme options are available?

You can choose from pre-designed themes (Default, Teal, Rose, Zinc, Vivid Pop, Aurora Mix) or create a custom theme. Each theme supports both light and dark modes. Access theme options in the Settings page.

### How do I enable accessibility features?

In Settings, you'll find accessibility options including high contrast mode, reduce motion, link underlining, and focus ring settings. Enable these features to improve your experience based on your specific needs.

### Can I change my notification preferences?

Yes, you can configure notification preferences in Settings. Choose which types of notifications you want to receive (email, in-app, push) and select the specific categories you're interested in (course updates, events, announcements, etc.).

### How do I change my password?

While you can reset a forgotten password from the sign-in page, regular password changes are typically handled through account settings or institutional policies. Contact your administrator if you need to change your password for security reasons.

## Events & Schedule

### How do I view upcoming events?

Access events through the Events page or your Schedule page. Both show upcoming events with details like date, time, location, and registration status. Your dashboard also shows a preview of upcoming events.

### What's the difference between personal, course, and global events?

Personal events are your own scheduled activities, course events are related to your enrolled courses, and global events are organization-wide activities. You can filter your calendar view to show specific event types.

### How do I add events to my personal calendar?

If you have permission, you can create personal events through the Schedule page. For course and global events, registration options may be available. Events can often be exported to external calendar applications like Google Calendar or Outlook.

### Can I set reminders for events?

Yes, configure event reminders in your notification settings. You can set multiple reminders at different intervals before an event. Reminders can be delivered via email, in-app notifications, or push notifications.

## Himpunan Features

### How do I access job opportunities (Loker)?

Browse job opportunities through the dedicated Loker section. You can filter listings by category, location, job type, and other criteria. Each listing provides details about the position, requirements, and application process.

### Are the job listings verified and legitimate?

While the platform strives to list legitimate opportunities, always verify job listings independently. Research companies, be cautious with personal information, and report suspicious listings to administrators. Legitimate jobs never require payment to apply.

### How do I use hotline forms?

Hotline forms are available for various purposes including technical support, feedback, service requests, and general inquiries. Select the appropriate form type, fill in required fields with detailed information, and submit. You'll receive responses through the platform or email.

### How can I improve my Hall of Fame ranking?

Focus on consistent, quality learning engagement. Regular study sessions, meaningful interaction with course content, and completing tryouts contribute to your learning time. Rankings are based on genuine learning activity, not just time accumulation.

### How often are announcements updated?

Announcements are posted as needed by administrators and instructors. Important updates may appear on your dashboard and trigger notifications. Check the Announcements page regularly to stay informed about platform news and updates.

## Scholarships

### How do I find scholarships that match my profile?

Browse the Scholarships section and use filtering options to narrow down opportunities based on criteria like field of study, academic achievement, financial need, or demographic factors. Review eligibility requirements carefully before applying.

### What documents do I typically need for scholarship applications?

Common requirements include academic transcripts, personal statements, letters of recommendation, financial information, and proof of enrollment. Specific requirements vary by scholarship - check each listing for detailed requirements.

### How can I track my scholarship application deadlines?

Add scholarship deadlines to your platform calendar and configure notification reminders. Many scholarships have strict deadlines, so plan to submit applications well before the due date.

### What should I do if I have questions about a specific scholarship?

Contact the scholarship provider directly using the contact information provided in the listing. For platform-related issues or if you can't reach the provider, use hotline forms for assistance.

## Technical Issues

### What browsers are supported by HMM LMS?

The platform supports modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, keep your browser updated to the latest version. JavaScript must be enabled for full functionality.

### Can I access the platform on mobile devices?

Yes, the platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices including smartphones and tablets. All major features are available on mobile, though some interfaces may be adapted for smaller screens.

### What should I do if the platform is running slowly?

Try clearing your browser cache, disabling browser extensions, or switching to a different browser. Check your internet connection speed. If problems persist, report the issue through hotline forms with details about your browser and device.

### How do I report a bug or technical issue?

Use the technical support hotline form to report bugs. Provide detailed information including what you were trying to do, what happened, any error messages you received, and your browser/device information. Screenshots are helpful.

## Account & Security

### How do I update my email address?

Email address changes are typically handled through your institution's administration for security reasons. Contact your administrator if you need to change your email address. This helps maintain account security and institutional records.

### Is my personal information secure on the platform?

Yes, your personal information is protected with industry-standard security measures. Data is encrypted during transmission and storage. Access to your information is limited to authorized personnel. Review the platform's privacy policy for detailed information.

### How can I tell if my account has been compromised?

Signs of compromise include unexpected password changes, unfamiliar activity in your account, or receiving notifications about actions you didn't take. If you suspect compromise, immediately change your password and contact support through hotline forms.

### Can I delete my account?

Account deletion is typically handled through institutional policies. Contact your administrator if you need to leave the platform. Your data may be retained according to institutional and legal requirements even after account access is removed.
