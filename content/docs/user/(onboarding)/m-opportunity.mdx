M-Opportunity lists job and internship openings shared with HMM ITB members.

## Finding an Opening

1. Select **M-Opportunity** on the sidebar.
2. Use **Search & filters** to find a role:
   - Search by job title or company.
   - Filter by **location** (e.g. Bandung, Jakarta).
   - Filter by **seniority** (intern, junior, mid, senior).
   - Filter by **employment type** (intern, part-time, full-time, contract).
   - Filter by the related engineering **stream**.
3. Click an opening to see its job information: company, position, level, type, salary, required streams, and when it was posted.

## Applying

Click **Apply Now** to apply through the link provided.

<Callout type="info">
  Applications go to the company or the link provided in the listing, not to hmmitb.com. Follow up with the company directly about your application.
</Callout>
