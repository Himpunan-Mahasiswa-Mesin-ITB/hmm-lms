## Himpunan Features

The Himpunan section encompasses community engagement features that connect you with the broader platform community. This includes announcements, job opportunities, hotline forms, and recognition programs.

<Callout type="info" title="Community Hub">
  Himpunan features are designed to keep you connected with news, opportunities, and community activities beyond your academic courses.
</Callout>

## Available Features

### 📢 **Announcements**
Stay updated with the latest news and updates:
- **Platform News**: Important platform updates and maintenance notices
- **Community Updates**: News relevant to the entire user community
- **Event Announcements**: Information about upcoming events and activities
- **Policy Changes**: Updates to platform policies and guidelines

### 💼 **Job Opportunities (Loker)**
Explore career and job opportunities:
- **Job Listings**: Browse available job vacancies
- **Filter Options**: Search by category, location, or type
- **Application Process**: Direct links to application procedures
- **Job Details**: Comprehensive information about each opportunity

### 📞 **Hotline Forms**
Access support and request forms:
- **Support Requests**: Submit issues and requests for help
- **Feedback Forms**: Provide feedback on platform features
- **Service Requests**: Request specific services or resources
- **Communication Channels**: Direct lines to support teams

### 🏆 **Hall of Fame**
Celebrate academic excellence and engagement:
- **Weekly Rankings**: Top learners based on learning activity
- **Leaderboard**: Competitive ranking system
- **Achievement Recognition**: Highlight top performers
- **Motivation**: Encourage healthy competition and engagement

## Troubleshooting

### 🐛 **Common Issues**
- **Announcements Not Loading**: Check your internet connection
- **Job Search Not Working**: Clear browser cache and try again
- **Forms Not Submitting**: Verify all required fields are completed
- **Hall of Fame Not Updating**: Rankings update weekly, not immediately

### 🔧 **Getting Help**
- **Technical Issues**: Use hotline forms to report problems
- **Content Questions**: Contact administrators for clarification
- **General Support**: Check our [FAQ section](/docs/user/faq)
- **Feedback**: Share suggestions for improving Himpunan features
