  The tryouts page groups tryouts by course. To access a course's tryouts, [enroll in the course](/docs/user/courses#enrolling-in-a-course) first.
</Callout>

## Taking a Tryout

1. Select **Tryouts** on the sidebar.
2. Choose the tryout you want to attempt. For example, to prepare for the 3rd partial exam of Heat Transfer, choose _Tryout UP3 Perpan_.
3. The tryout details page shows the question overview, the time limit, whether you can attempt it more than once, and whether you can view the correct answers afterwards. Click **Start New Attempt**. The timer starts immediately.
4. The questions are shown along with their answer choices or an answer box.
5. Use the **Navigator** to jump between questions and see which ones you have answered. Your answers are saved as you go.
6. Finish the tryout before the time limit runs out. When time is up, the tryout is submitted automatically.
7. Click **Submit** once you have answered all questions, then confirm.

<Callout type="warn">
  Submitting cannot be undone. Check the **Navigator** for unanswered questions before you confirm.
</Callout>

## Viewing Your Results

After submitting, the results page shows:

- Your **score**.
- The number of **questions answered**.
- The **time taken**.

If the tryout allows it, the **Question Review** shows the correct answer and an explanation for each question.

## Continuing an Attempt

If you leave in the middle of an attempt, you can continue it from the tryout details page, which marks it as **In Progress**. The timer keeps running while you are away, so return before the time limit runs out.
