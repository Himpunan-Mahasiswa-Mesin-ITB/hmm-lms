## Your Personal Dashboard

The dashboard is your central hub for learning and engagement on the HMM LMS platform. It provides a comprehensive overview of your learning progress, upcoming events, and quick access to important features.

<Callout type="info" title="Pro Tip">
  Access your dashboard anytime by clicking on the Dashboard link in the sidebar navigation.
</Callout>

## Dashboard Features

### 🎯 **Weekly Learning Focus**

The top section of your dashboard displays your weekly learning focus with:

- **Personalized Message**: Motivational content to keep you engaged
- **Quick Actions**: Direct buttons to continue learning and view upcoming events
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual representation of your learning streak

### 📚 **Continue Your Courses**

This section shows your active enrolled courses with:

- **Course List**: Up to 4 of your most recent courses
- **Course Details**: Title, class code, video count, and materials count
- **Quick Access**: Direct links to continue learning
- **Empty State**: Guidance when you're not enrolled in any courses

### 📊 **Learning Analytics**

View your learning progress through detailed charts:

- **Activity Charts**: Visual representation of your learning patterns
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your completion rates
- **Time Management**: See how you're spending your learning time

### 👑 **Hall of Fame**

Compete with fellow learners and track your ranking:

- **Weekly Leaderboard**: Top 3 learners of the week
- **Learning Statistics**: Minutes spent learning this week
- **Full Ranking**: Access to complete leaderboard
- **Achievement Recognition**: Trophy icons for top performers

### 📅 **Calendar Integration**

Your personal calendar shows:

- **Upcoming Events**: Both personal and course-related events
- **Deadlines**: Important dates and submission deadlines
- **Schedule Overview**: Monthly view of your activities
- **Event Details**: Quick access to event information

## Dashboard Statistics

Track your overall progress with key metrics:

### 📈 **Learning Statistics**

- **Active Courses**: Number of courses you're currently enrolled in
- **Weekly Learning Minutes**: Total time spent learning this week
- **Tryout Attempts**: Number of assessments you've completed

### 🏆 **Achievement Tracking**

- **Learning Streak**: Consecutive days of learning activity
- **Completion Rates**: Percentage of course content completed
- **Ranking Position**: Your position in the weekly leaderboard

## Dashboard Best Practices

### 🎯 **Maximize Your Learning**

1. **Check Daily**: Visit your dashboard every day to maintain your learning streak
2. **Review Progress**: Use the analytics to identify areas for improvement
3. **Plan Ahead**: Use the calendar to prepare for upcoming events and deadlines
4. **Stay Competitive**: Monitor the Hall of Fame to stay motivated

### 📊 **Understanding Your Analytics**

- **High Activity Weeks**: Identify your most productive learning periods
- **Course Progress**: See which courses need more attention
- **Time Management**: Optimize your learning schedule based on your patterns

### 🔔 **Stay Updated**

- **Event Notifications**: Check the calendar regularly for new events
- **Course Updates**: Monitor your courses for new content and materials
- **Announcements**: Stay informed about platform updates and news

## Customizing Your Dashboard Experience

While the dashboard provides a standardized view, you can enhance your experience through:

- **Theme Selection**: Choose colors that match your preference in Settings
- **Display Settings**: Adjust font sizes and spacing for better readability
- **Notification Preferences**: Configure alerts for important updates

## Troubleshooting

### Dashboard Not Loading

- Refresh your browser page
- Check your internet connection
- Clear your browser cache

### Missing Courses

- Ensure you're properly enrolled in courses
- Check with your instructor if courses should be visible
- Verify your account status and permissions

### Calendar Events Not Showing

- Ensure you have the proper event permissions
- Check that events are within the current date range
- Verify your event filter settings

If you encounter issues with your dashboard or need assistance understanding any features, refer to our [FAQ section](/docs/user/faq) or contact support through the available channels.
