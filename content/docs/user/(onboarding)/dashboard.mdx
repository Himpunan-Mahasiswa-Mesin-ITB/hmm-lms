The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It summarizes your learning activity and what's coming up. You can always return to it by selecting **Dashboard** in the **General** section of the sidebar.

## What's on the Dashboard

![User Dashboard](/images/docs/user-guide/dashboard.png)

1. **Weekly Learning Focus**. Shortcuts to **Continue Learning** (opens [Courses](/docs/user/courses)) and **Upcoming Events** (opens [Events](/docs/user/events)).
2. **Profile Progress**. If you have been assigned one or more progress profiles, this card shows your overall progress percentage. Click a group to expand it and see the progress of each profile in it, along with when it was last updated. Click **Add Standalone Profile** to search for and add a standalone profile you want to track, or **Remove Standalone Profile** to stop tracking one.

![User Dashboard 2](/images/docs/user-guide/dashboard2.png)

3. **Continue Your Courses**. Up to four of your enrolled courses, each showing the class code and its number of videos and materials. Click a course to open it, or click **View all** to go to the Courses page. If you haven't enrolled in any course yet, click **Browse Courses** to find one.
4. **Activity Minutes**. A bar chart of how many minutes you spent learning on the website on each of the last seven days.
5. **Hall of Fame**. The top three learners of the week. Click **View full ranking** to open the [Hall of Fame](/docs/user/hall-of-fame) page.
6. **Calendar**. A small calendar where days with events or forms are marked.

<Callout type="info" title="Profile Progress not showing?">
  The Profile Progress card only appears once you have been assigned a progress profile, or after
  you add a standalone profile yourself.
</Callout>

## Using the Calendar

Select a date on the calendar to see that day's events and forms:

- Click an **event** to see its time, location, and number of attendees. From there you can:
  - **RSVP** (**Will Attend** / **Won't Attend**) before the RSVP deadline.
  - **Check In** to record your attendance (available from one hour before the event starts).
  - Click **View Details** to open the event page.
- Click a **form** to read its description and open it, as long as the form is currently open.

For a full-size calendar with filters and more views, use the [Schedule](/docs/user/schedule) page.
