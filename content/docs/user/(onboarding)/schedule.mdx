The schedule page contains a calendar with upcoming activities, such as events held by HMM ITB, exam schedules, and course activities.

![Image 4](/images/docs/user-guide/schedule.png)

## Viewing the Schedule

1. Select **Schedule** on the sidebar.
2. Use the **Filter** button to choose which events are shown:
   - **Personal**: only visible to you.
   - **Course**: from courses you are enrolled in.
   - **Global**: HMM ITB-wide events.
3. Change the calendar view to show the events of a **month**, a **week**, a **day**, or a **list** (agenda).
4. Click an event on the calendar to see its details.

<Callout type="info" title="Read-only for users">
  Only admins can create, edit, and delete events on the schedule. To RSVP or check in to an event,
  open it from the [Events](/docs/user/events) page or the calendar on your
  [Dashboard](/docs/user/dashboard).
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea" title="Missing a course event?">
  Course events only show up for courses you are enrolled in. See [Courses](/docs/user/courses) to
  enroll.
</Callout>
