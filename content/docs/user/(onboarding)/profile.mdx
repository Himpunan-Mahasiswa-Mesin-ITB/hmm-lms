## Opening Your Profile

Open your profile by clicking your name at the bottom of the sidebar and selecting **Profile**.

The profile page shows:

- Your **personal information**: name, NIM, email, faculty, program, and position.
- Your **learning statistics**: active courses, tryout attempts, and weekly learning minutes.

## Editing Your Profile

1. Click **Edit Profile** on the profile page.
2. Change your profile photo and cover photo (you can crop the image before saving).
3. Update your full name and position.
4. Save your changes.

<Callout type="info">
  Your NIM, email, faculty, and program cannot be changed.
</Callout>

## Changing Your Password

1. Click **Edit Profile** on the profile page.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Enter your new password twice.
4. Save your changes.

Forgot your current password? Sign out and use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page instead. See the [FAQ](/docs/user/faq#i-forgot-my-password).

## Signing Out

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Sign Out**.
