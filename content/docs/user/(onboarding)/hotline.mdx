Hotline is where you can submit reports and requests to HMM ITB.

## Submitting a Report

1. Select **Hotline** on the sidebar.
2. You will see the active hotline forms, each with information about its reporting flow.
3. To submit a report, click **Open Form** on the topic you want to report and fill it in.

Read the reporting flow on the form's card before you submit, so you know how your report will be handled.
