## Reading Announcements

1. Select **Announcements** on the sidebar.
2. You will see the latest announcements about HMM ITB activities. Click **See more** to open an announcement in full.

## Replying

Reply to an announcement by clicking **Reply**, writing your message, and sending it. All replies are shown on the announcement's page.

## Course Announcements

Announcements for a specific course are shown in the **Recent Announcements** section of that course's page. See [Courses](/docs/user/courses#inside-a-course).
