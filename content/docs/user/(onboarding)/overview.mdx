## Platform Overview

Welcome to the HMM LMS user documentation! This platform provides a comprehensive learning management system designed to enhance your educational experience through various features and tools.

## Available Features

As a user, you have access to the following main features:

### 📚 **Academic Features**

- **Course Management**: Browse, enroll in, and access your courses
- **Learning Progress**: Track your progress through videos, materials, and lessons
- **Tryouts & Assessments**: Take quizzes and assessments to test your knowledge
- **Scholarships**: View and apply for available scholarship opportunities
- **Academic Resources**: Access course materials, documents, and learning resources

### 📅 **Calendar & Scheduling**

- **Personal Calendar**: View your personal events and deadlines
- **Course Events**: Stay updated with course-related schedules
- **Global Events**: Access organization-wide events and activities
- **Schedule Management**: Plan and organize your learning timeline

### 👤 **Profile & Account**

- **Profile Management**: Update your personal information, avatar, and cover image
- **Academic Information**: Manage your faculty, program, and position details
- **Learning Statistics**: Track your active courses, weekly learning minutes, and tryout attempts
- **Account Settings**: Customize your experience with various preferences

### 🎨 **Personalization**

- **Theme Selection**: Choose from multiple color schemes (Default, Teal, Rose, Zinc, Vivid Pop, Aurora Mix)
- **Custom Themes**: Create your own color theme with advanced customization
- **Display Settings**: Adjust font family, font size, and spacing
- **Accessibility Options**: Configure high contrast, reduce motion, underline links, and focus ring settings
- **Notification Preferences**: Manage your notification settings

### 🏆 **Engagement & Recognition**

- **Hall of Fame**: View weekly top learners and compete for rankings
- **Learning Analytics**: Track your learning progress with detailed charts and statistics
- **Achievement Tracking**: Monitor your learning streak and accomplishments

### 📢 **Himpunan Features**

- **Announcements**: Stay updated with latest news and announcements
- **Events**: Browse and participate in upcoming events
- **Job Opportunities**: View and apply for job vacancies (Loker)
- **Hotline Forms**: Access various hotline forms for support and requests

### 🔧 **Additional Features**

- **Dashboard**: Central hub with quick access to courses, events, and learning progress
- **Search & Navigation**: Easily find content using the search functionality
- **Responsive Design**: Access the platform from any device
- **Real-time Updates**: Get instant updates on courses, events, and announcements
