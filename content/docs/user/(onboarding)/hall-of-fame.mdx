The Hall of Fame ranks the users who spent the most time learning on hmmitb.com this week. Select **Hall of Fame** in the **General** section of the sidebar to open it.

## How the Ranking Works

- The **top three** are shown on a podium.
- The rest of the **top 20** are listed below, along with their learning minutes for the week.
- The ranking **resets every week**.

Learning minutes come from the time you spend studying course content (see [Courses](/docs/user/courses)). The same minutes appear in the **Activity Minutes** chart on your [Dashboard](/docs/user/dashboard) and in the statistics on your [Profile](/docs/user/profile).

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  The dashboard shows the top three of the week. Click **View full ranking** there to jump to this page.
</Callout>
